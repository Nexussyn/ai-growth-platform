How it works

From signal to settlement.

A continuous loop discovers real revenue opportunities worldwide, scores them with an external intelligence, validates demand before activating, and settles on-chain — routing around any failure.

🔭

Discover

An always-on scout searches the world — in every language — for real revenue signals: bounties, commissions, automation fees, data, licensing and beyond.

🧠

Score

Each opportunity is analysed by a real external intelligence across 8 metrics (value, demand, cost, risk, margin, automation…) and scored.

✅

Validate

Nothing is "active" until it shows real evidence of demand and a path to automated collection. No proof → it waits in the backlog.

⛓️

Settle

Confirmed USDC payments on Base are watched on-chain and split 75/25 automatically — gross, immutable, owner-first.
